<Color id="nord_accent">Revolutions per minute (RPM)</Color> determines how *fast* a machine, like the <ItemImage id="create:mechanical_piston" scale="0.70" /> <ItemLink id="create:mechanical_piston" /> or <ItemImage id="create:mechanical_press" scale="0.70" /> <ItemLink id="create:mechanical_press" />, operates at thus increasing a machine's throughput: **Throughput ∝ RPM**.

<Color id="nord_accent">Stress unit (SU)</Color> can be seen alike as power/RF/FE. Generators such as <ItemImage id="create:water_wheel" scale="0.70" /> <ItemLink id="create:water_wheel" /> or <ItemImage id="create:steam_engine" scale="0.70" /> <ItemLink id="create:steam_engine" /> generate SU. Whilst consumers require SU to function. Generally, you can say **SU_consumed = Base Value × RPM**. Running out of SU causes your entire network to lock up and freeze.
